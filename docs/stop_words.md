## Stop words - Text preprocessing 

Stop words are common words in a language, such as “the,” “is,” “and,” or “of,” that usually carry little meaning on their own and are often removed before text analysis. In natural language processing (NLP), they are commonly filtered out because they appear very frequently and do not help much in distinguishing one document or sentence from another. This preprocessing step can reduce noise, shorten the size of the vocabulary, and improve the efficiency of algorithms such as bag-of-words, topic modeling, or information retrieval. However, removing stop words also has drawbacks: in some tasks, such as sentiment analysis, machine translation, or phrase-based understanding, words like “not” or “no” are crucial because they can completely change the meaning of a sentence. Therefore, while stop words are useful for simplifying and speeding up NLP pipelines, they must be used carefully, since deleting them blindly can lead to loss of important context and weaker model performance in certain applications.
